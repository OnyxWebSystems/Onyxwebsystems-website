03 · Pricing

Two numbers

Every BOS deal has two layers. Never one.

LayerWhat it isHow you calculate it
1. Module fee (13%)Price to put that function into the operating system13% of what that role would cost as an employee for one year
2. Monthly retainerOngoing run, improve, support, keep the system aliveSized to how big the project is — not a flat fee, and not 13% again

Part 1 — The 13% module fee

  1. Name the job the module is doing.
  2. Get the annual salary for that seat.
  3. Charge 13% of that annual amount.
Charge = annual salary of that role × 0.13

Sales module

R240,000 × 0.13 = R31,200

Finance module

R180,000 × 0.13 = R23,400

Several modules: calculate 13% per module, then add. A full operating system is a stack, not a mystery lump sum.

“Instead of hiring another person to run this all year, we put that function in the system. The module fee is 13% of what that seat would cost you annually. Then there is a monthly retainer, sized to the size of the build.”

03 · Retainer

Monthly retainer

Sized to how big the project is.

The 13% is not the whole relationship. The retainer is not 13% again. It is not the same for every client.

What it pays for

  • Keeping the system live
  • Improving workflows as the business changes
  • Support when staff get stuck
  • Channel monitoring (WhatsApp, web, Instagram — depending on the build)
  • Knowledge updates and small scope that is not a new module
  • Reporting the client actually uses
SignalSmallerBigger
ModulesOne moduleSeveral connected modules
Team1–10 people11–50 / 51–200 / 200+
ChannelsOne formPhone, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, email
WorkflowsOne simple pathApprovals, payments, multi-location
CustomStandard moduleCustom agents and specialised systems

Small → lower monthly. Mid (two or three modules, 11–50 staff, WhatsApp + web) → mid. Large (full OS, many channels, custom) → highest. Until leadership gives exact bands, size the project correctly. Do not invent a rand amount on the call.

Websites and apps are scoped, then quoted. Do not apply 13% of a designer’s salary unless leadership says so.
